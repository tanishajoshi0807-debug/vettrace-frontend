About Pashu Aarogya

Pashu Aarogya is a digital livestock health and compliance platform designed to improve livestock health management and traceability.

It helps veterinarians, farmers, collectors and regulators manage animal health records, track antimicrobial usage and monitor withdrawal periods.

Our Features

Everything you need for better livestock health management and compliance.

Animal Health Records

Maintain digital records of animal health, treatments, medicines and medical history.

Antimicrobial Tracking

Track antimicrobial medicines administered to livestock and maintain complete records.

Withdrawal Monitoring

Monitor withdrawal periods and identify animals that are safe for collection.

Compliance Monitoring

Help stakeholders maintain livestock health standards and regulatory compliance.

How It Works

Pashu Aarogya connects all livestock stakeholders through a simple digital workflow.

1

Register Animal

Register livestock and add important identification and health information.

2

Record Treatment

Veterinarians record medicines, treatments and antimicrobial usage.

3

Monitor Withdrawal

The system tracks the required withdrawal period after treatment.

4

Verify Compliance

Collectors and regulators can verify whether livestock meets compliance requirements.
